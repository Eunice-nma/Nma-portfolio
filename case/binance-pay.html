The problem

Card restrictions in Algeria sent users to Bitnob's virtual card, but deposits only worked for Nigeria and mobile-money countries. Users relied on workarounds, and support was flooded with requests for Binance Pay.

How it came together

Built for the people using it

Testing with Algerian users showed English instructions were a barrier, so I added an Arabic and French switch. The main button uses Binance's yellow, so the flow is easy to follow even without reading.

Usage took off straight away

The deposit screen went from almost no views to about 20,000 a month after launch, and stayed there.

A new revenue market

Algeria had no revenue in August and September 2023. Within months it was second only to Nigeria.

Results

  • Over $50,000 in transactions in the first two months.
  • Virtual card revenue grew 30%.
  • Deposit questions from Algeria dropped sharply.
  • The whole app was later translated into Arabic, French and Swahili.

What I learned

  • Language is part of the product, not an afterthought.
  • Support tickets are some of the best product research you can get.
