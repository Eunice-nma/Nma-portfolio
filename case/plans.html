The problem

BTC Plans was one of Bitnob's earliest and most-used features. However, because it worked well, it hadn't received much attention over time. Its interface had become outdated, and users had no way to access their funds before maturity without ending their plans entirely. This created an opportunity to improve the overall experience, introduce more flexibility, and increase the feature's contribution to revenue.

How it came together

Finding untapped potential

BTC Plans was one of Bitnob's earliest features. It had been working well, so it hadn't received much attention over time. However, Mixpanel data showed that it remained one of our most-used features despite contributing little to revenue. We saw an opportunity to revisit the experience and unlock more value from it.

Simplifying the savings experience

We reworked the Plans interface, removing unnecessary elements and improving the overall flow. We also made earnings and losses more visible, helping users understand how their savings were performing without having to dig through the interface.

Introducing more flexibility

Users previously had to wait until their plans matured to access their funds. We introduced early withdrawals, giving users more control over their savings while introducing a withdrawal fee that created a new revenue stream for the feature.

Making the costs clear

Introducing withdrawal fees meant users needed to understand what they would be charged before making a decision. We made the applicable fees clearer within the withdrawal flow, helping users make informed decisions and avoid unexpected charges.

Results

  • BTC Plans became revenue-generating for the first time.
  • More users kept their plans running to the due date.

What I learned

  • The best way to make money from a feature is to solve a real user problem at the same time.
